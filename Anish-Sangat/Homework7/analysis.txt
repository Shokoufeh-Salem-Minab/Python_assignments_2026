SALES DATA ANALYSIS - ANSWERS

1. Which product generated the highest revenue?
   Phone generated the highest revenue: $22,780.00
   (49.1% of total revenue of $46,363.00).

2. Which category generated the most revenue?
   The Mobile category generated the most revenue: $22,780.00
   (49.1% of total revenue).

3. Which region performed best?
   The South region performed best with $13,368.00
   in sales (28.8% of total revenue).

4. What can you conclude from your analysis?
   - High-priced products drive revenue more than high volume. Phone was not the
     best-selling item by units, yet it earned the most because of its high price.
   - Mouse sold the most units (46), but because it is cheap it
     contributes little revenue. Mouse earned the least revenue overall ($1,145.00).
   - The Mobile category is the main source of income, so the business should
     keep strong stock and promotion for these products.
   - Revenue is spread across all regions, but South leads. Weaker regions could be
     targeted with marketing or promotions.
   - Most individual sales are small (low revenue per record), with a few large sales
     (Laptops and Phones) pulling the total up, as seen in the histogram.
