Sales Data Analysis - Interpretation

1. Which product generated the highest revenue?
Phone generated the highest revenue with 22,580.00 in total, ahead of
Laptop (16,570.00).

2. Which category generated the most revenue?
Electronics generated the most revenue with 39,150.00, which is about
80% of the total revenue of 49,118.00. Accessories generated 9,968.00.

3. Which region performed best?
North performed best with 14,645.00 in revenue. South was second
(12,975.00), followed by East (11,990.00) and West (9,508.00).

4. What can you conclude from your analysis?
Revenue is driven by price, not by volume. Mouse was the best-selling
product by quantity (80 units) but produced very little revenue because
each unit is cheap. Phones and Laptops sold far fewer units (28 and 14)
but together make up most of the revenue, so Electronics is the key
category for the business. North is the strongest region, mainly
because it recorded the largest single sale (10 Phones, 7,800.00), while
West is the weakest and could use more sales effort. Headphones are a
solid middle option, earning more than Keyboards and Mice combined.
