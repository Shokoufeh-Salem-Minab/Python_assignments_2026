Sales Data Analysis - Assignment 7
==================================

1. Which product generated the highest revenue?
   Phone generated the highest total revenue: EUR 14,320.00.

2. Which category generated the most revenue?
   Electronics generated EUR 30,240.00, making it the highest-revenue category.

3. Which region performed best?
   South performed best, with total sales revenue of EUR 11,475.00.

4. What can you conclude from your analysis?
   The dataset produced total revenue of EUR 37,110.00. Electronics accounts
   for most of this revenue, mainly because laptops and phones have much higher
   prices than accessories. Mice had the greatest quantity sold (53 units),
   but their low unit price means they generated less revenue than phones.
   Focusing promotion and stock availability on high-value electronics,
   especially in the South region, should support revenue growth.
