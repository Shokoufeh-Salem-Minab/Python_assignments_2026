# Sales Analysis
#
# Which product generated the highest revenue?
# Phone, with 10450 in revenue (37.6% of the total 27765). Laptop is close behind with 9050.
#
# Which category generated the most revenue?
# Computers, with 12900 (46.5%). It is the top category even though Phone is the top single
# product, because it combines Laptop and Tablet sales. Mobile has 10450 and Accessories 4415.
#
# Which region performed best?
# North, with 10500 in revenue. That is much more than the other regions, which are all
# close to each other: East 6010, South 5980, West 5275.
#
# What can you conclude from your analysis?
# Revenue depends on price much more than on quantity. Mouse sold the most units (47) but made
# one of the lowest revenues (1135), while Laptop sold only 9 units and made 9050. Expensive
# products (Phone, Laptop, Tablet) bring in about 84% of the revenue. The histogram shows that
# most single sales are small (under 1000) and only a few big sales bring most of the money.
# North is the strongest region, mainly because of the biggest single sale (7 phones, 4550).
# To grow revenue it makes sense to focus on expensive products and to look at why the other
# regions sell less than North.
