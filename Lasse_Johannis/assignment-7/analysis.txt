1. Which product generated the highest revenue?
The Phone generated the highest revenue with 16100 EUR (23 phones sold at 700 EUR).
The Laptop is second with 12350 EUR.

2. Which category generated the most revenue?
Mobile generated the most revenue with 16100 EUR, which is 44.7% of the total revenue
of 36045 EUR. Computers (Laptop and Tablet) are close behind with 15050 EUR (41.8%).

3. Which region performed best?
The South region performed best with 11460 EUR, followed by North with 10720 EUR.
East (7650 EUR) and West (6215 EUR) are clearly lower.

4. What can you conclude from your analysis?
Most of the revenue (about 79%) comes from the two expensive products, phones and
laptops. The Mouse is the most sold product with 47 pieces, but because it only
costs 25 EUR it brings the lowest revenue (1175 EUR). So a high quantity does not mean
a high revenue - the price matters more. The histogram also shows that most single
sales are small (under 1200 EUR) and only a few big laptop and phone sales bring the
high values. To increase revenue, the shop should focus on phones and laptops,
especially in the East and West regions where sales are lower.
